04
What we automated there
One path · three steps

Onboarding. KYC. Invoice.

01
Onboarding
Talks to the client on the channel they already use — email, WhatsApp, whatever is convenient. Understands what they need, which entity, which work — then sends them into KYC.
02
KYC
Builds and parses the document list for the engagement. For accounting, that list depends on the company’s emirate. Collects the files, flags what is missing, and issues the engagement letter.
03
Invoice
Once the scope is clear, it prepares and raises the invoice. A person still confirms the number and the send.

The 80% is overall manual hours in that engagement — not a per-workflow result, and not a forecast for Across Legal.

05
The human seat
The rule in every build

Agents prepare.
People decide.

Nothing binding, legal, or client-facing leaves without a named person. Software does not make a firm compliant.

Stays with the lawyer
KYC sufficiency, AML risk, whether the matter proceeds, any report, any send.
Moves to the agent
Collect, classify, spot gaps, structure facts, assemble the pack, draft the follow-up.
Always named
A process owner reviews exceptions. The system never silently passes a missing file.
